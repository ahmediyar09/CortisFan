About

The story so far.

Who they are

CORTIS is a five-member South Korean boy group formed by BIGHIT MUSIC (HYBE) — the label’s third boy group after BTS and TXT. They describe themselves as a “young creator crew”: all members take part in songwriting, production, choreography and video work.

The name

CORTIS is built from six letters of the phrase COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES — a call to think freely and break free from the world’s standards.

Debut

The group debuted on August 18, 2025 with the single “What You Want”, followed by the debut EP COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES on September 8, 2025. Its songs were developed at a song camp in Los Angeles. In May 2026 came the second EP, GREENGREEN.
